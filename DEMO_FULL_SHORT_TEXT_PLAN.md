# MI-BTrack Full Demo and Short Demo Text Plan

## 1. Plan decision

The demo will contain only two tour choices:

1. **Full Demo**
2. **Short Demo**

The tour supports Marathi, Hindi and English. Marathi remains the authoritative wording copied from the supplied document. Hindi and English follow the same 23 steps and are aligned translations of that wording; they must not add, omit or reorder any instruction.

All Marathi explanations and all example field values below come from the supplied Word documents. The Marathi wording used inside the demo must remain exactly as written here. Hindi and English may translate this wording, but no language may introduce newly invented narration.

The four supplied documents are not four consecutive parts. They are overlapping versions of the same script. `Document by Pratiksha for the AI Model.docx` is the newest and most complete version. It contains the complete content of `(1)` and adds the Reports section. `(2)` and `Modelss` are shorter drafts with formatting differences. The complete version is therefore the source for this plan.

The supplied documents define the Full Demo content. They do not contain a separately written Short Demo. The Short Demo will therefore reuse selected Full Demo steps without changing their text.

## 2. Tour presentation plan

### 2.1 Dashboard choices

The dashboard tour area will show only:

- Full Demo
- Short Demo

Language is selected inside either tour, so the dashboard still has only two choices. Playback speed remains available at 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 3×.

### 2.2 Text display

For each step:

- Open the required real page.
- Highlight the relevant menu, button, form or report.
- Show the explanation for the selected language, aligned to the exact Marathi source assigned to that step.
- Type or select the exact example values assigned to that step.
- Keep Previous, Next, Pause or Resume, Restart and Exit available for navigation.
- Play the matching Marathi, Hindi or English narration clip for the selected language.
- Keep autotyping, highlighting and narration synchronized at every playback speed.

### 2.3 Content types

Each Full Demo step below has up to three parts:

- **Page and action** identifies the screen and interaction.
- **Text to display** is the exact explanatory text from the document.
- **Demo values** are the exact values to type or select.
