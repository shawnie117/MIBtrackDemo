## Recommended order on a free allowance

The Full Demo is the source pack. Short Demo reuses selected clips,
so it never needs duplicate generation. Record in this order:

1. **Marathi Full Demo**, F01 through F23.
2. Verify every clip in the player at 1× and 2×.
3. Short Demo automatically reuses its selected Full Demo clips.

Until a clip exists the player speaks that step with the browser voice, so
a partly recorded pack is still fully demonstrable. Nothing breaks.

## Licensing - please read before recording

At the time of writing, ElevenLabs free-plan output carries **no commercial
rights**, requires attribution if published, and upgrading later does not
retroactively license audio that was generated while on the free plan.

So:

- Use free-plan audio for internal review and rehearsal only.
- Regenerate the approved final pack under a paid plan before showing it to
  a customer or publishing it.
- Do not assume an upgrade covers what you already made.

Verify the current terms yourself before the commercial recording:

- [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing)
- [Can I publish the content I generate?](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform)
- [Model overview and supported languages](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models)

_Terms above were summarised from those pages and may have changed._
