After that we can take proper follow-up of the customer, so that we can provide timely services to that customer. By clicking on the Follow-Up button, we can add any service we want to provide in the Follow-Up Details. In its follow-up status we can make Pending. After that, we can manage the follow-up by using whatever option is suitable for us like Routine in Follow-Up For and Visit in Follow-Up Medium. After that whatever date and time we set in Next Follow-Up Date and Time, on that date we will see the name of that customer in Pending Follow-Ups on the dashboard. So that you know that this customer needs a follow-up today.
