In MI B-Track CRM we can also view visualized reports or analysis reports. For that, click on Reports on the left side and select Daily Analysis Report. In Daily Analysis Report we can see the Daily Report of our Particular Employee. Here, we can see how many leads they have generated today, how many leads they have followed up on, how many follow-ups they have pending, how many tickets they have resolved, how many payments they have collected, all this can be seen in the Daily Analysis Report. In that we can also see the particular report of that employee for a month or a few days.
